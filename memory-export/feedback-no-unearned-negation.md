name: no-unearned-negation
description: "Standing writing rule: recommend what to do; don't use negation or 'not X' contrasts unless the negation is earned"
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

State what to do. Avoid "don't X", "never X", "you are doing it wrong" and "use A, not B" framings unless the negation carries information a positive statement cannot (for example, correcting a widespread misconception). Cut contrasts like "select on Dying, not Dead": Say "Select on Dying" and give the reason.

**Why:** the user (2026-09-29, PR 23427 review, following a reviewer's "we should recommend what you should do, not what you shouldn't") made this a standing rule for all docs, comments, commit messages and PR text.
**How to apply:** check any prose before writing or presenting it; rewrite negative guidance positively. Related: [[capital-after-colon]], [[juju-pr-workflow]].
