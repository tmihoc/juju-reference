# Story form reconciliation -- session 110

## Decision and limit

The current entity section roster is useful for storage, streams, API calls and migration, but it is not an exhaustive grammar for the running subject's story. Treat these as conditional content roles, not required headings: definition and boundary; shared vocabulary and behavior; interactions (actor, conditions, results and errors); observation; persistence when an inspector needs it; and cross-cutting effects. Do not organize by audience or code package. This is a proposed rule, not a proven complete list or an approved structural change.

E49 was pre-registered in `structure-discovery-plan.md` before these source checks. Its prediction **passes the narrow registered criterion**: the cases distinguish at least one competing placement. They do not uniquely decide whether secret references belong in a short lede or a named topic section; the registered criterion did not require that. Three deliberately selected examples cannot establish global completeness, user benefit, or unique headings. Source tracing is not a live-controller test.

## Proposed reconciliation, for authorization before edits

1. Keep the established names and conditional order for the entity schema, change streams, calls and model-migration exception, including per-call `Caller`, `Rules`, `Errors`. They answer real questions; they are not a complete inventory of possible sections.
2. Allow a subject-specific vocabulary or shared-behavior section when the same fact is needed across calls or is observable independently of storage, and cannot fit the lede without obscuring it. Use an informative subject title, not a generic mandatory `Identity` or `Errors` section. For the secret example, a candidate is **Secret references**, with the existing `secret-uri`, `secret-name` and `secret-label` anchors retained; verify each definition before moving it. This is a proposed exception to any interpretation of the current identity-section ban that forbids a needed topic-specific section, not a claim that the present rules already authorize it.
3. Replace the blanket service-rule-to-call wording with: a call-specific rule stays with its call; a rule shared by multiple calls or observed independently has one subject or mechanism home, and the calls link to it. A storage section states database-enforced rules, not all rules whose facts happen to be stored. Resolve a genuine competing owner by a named reader question and narrowest subject that owns the common behavior; record unresolved ties rather than inventing certainty.
4. Correct both hook universal-chain sentences against the code, preserving a link to watchers for the applicable change-driven paths and the specific `update-status` description. Do not infer or enumerate other initiating paths from this one exception. This is a proposed content fix, not applied.
5. Correct the rules' `juju dump-db` example only after tracing the documentation context: at the anchor, the documented server path returns `NotImplemented` (`apiserver/facades/client/modelmanager/modelmanager.go:566-573`). `juju-db-repl` and backups remain separately assessed. Do not present this as a live verification.

No docs page, checker, counter selection or PR text changed in this research. The existing section order, lede limit and identity ban are user decisions; any exception needs approval. The selected examples show a constructive reconciliation and a false generalization, not a proven universal grammar.

## Bounded route to a domain-wide universe

An interface inventory is a discovery instrument, not a page list. Inventory supported reader surfaces as distinct ledgers: (1) CLI commands, options, output and errors, including relevant hook commands; (2) registered facade names/versions and actually callable methods, requests, responses, errors and watcher deliveries; (3) HTTP/control-socket endpoints; (4) charm metadata, hooks, hook environment and hook commands; (5) status and operator logs; (6) genuinely usable DB inspection and backup surfaces; (7) externally observed cross-cutting processes such as migration and upgrade. Include conditional registration, permissions, platform/provider variation and generated/dynamic artifacts. Exclude tests, mocks and internal-only interfaces; follow actual wiring and distinguish known registrations from usable contracts.

Known entry points are examples, not a completeness claim: `cmd/juju/commands/main.go:215-266,331-...` registers built-in commands and supports plugins; `apiserver/allfacades.go` imports facade packages and registers facade sets; `apiserver/apiserver.go:655-669` registers collected HTTP endpoints and other handlers may register dynamically; `internal/worker/uniter/runner/jujuc/server.go:39-141` composes enabled hook/action commands; `domain/deployment/charm/hooks/hooks.go:9-105,107-123` names hook kinds but a declaration alone does not prove one is emitted. Trace supported actual runtime paths rather than counting names. Status, log, DB/backup and metadata inventories need their own roots and explicit coverage checks.

For each discovered surface item, record source and runtime reachability, named audience question, subject, current owner or absence, and disposition: shared story, call-specific contract, observation, persistence, cross-cutting mechanism, linked out, or excluded with reason. Treat a surface item as a lead, not automatically a relevant fact or page. Reconcile intersecting surfaces against the same subject and one-owner rule; retain gaps and ambiguous owners. A bounded coverage claim requires roots and dynamic exceptions documented, every item disposed and independently checked, and the relevant configurations stated. An inventory still does not prove the roles complete or reader benefit; a content-controlled reader test would be separate.
