## Proposed shared contributor and bot guidance

Replace the ownership premise in `docs/agents/AGENTS.documentation.rules.md:465` **only after approval**: “A reader question tests whether a true, observable fact is relevant and covered; it does not determine ownership, page boundaries, or order. Derive the fact's subject or interaction from the behavior and its scope. Put shared behavior at its subject or mechanism address, call-specific conditions at the call, and link affected subjects. Record unresolved ties; a semantic address may be an anchor rather than a file.” This reconciles line 441's inclusion test and line 449's single-home practice with the later user decision. Do not replace source evidence with questions or present one-home as a discovered property.

Maintain the user's order of records, streams, actor, interactions, configuration and failure indexing as applicable, with a definition first and prerequisites introduced before use. Do not prescribe a database→watcher chain as universal. Permit subject-specific shared vocabulary and behavior where needed, without a mandatory generic heading; preserve anchors on moves. For each claim specify applicable subject, scope, actor, prerequisites, result, timing, conditional platform/provider behavior and supported observable surface when needed; distinguish request accepted from achieved state. The E59 disk case shows independently varying internals do not by themselves satisfy inclusion. Do not claim that the causal order or one-home convention improves reader performance without a content-controlled test.

Human and bot use the same source-backed rules. A mechanical checker can verify anchor existence/uniqueness, link resolution, prescribed syntax, explicitly scoped source manifests and documented-message template membership. It cannot determine semantic ownership, authorization/reachability, condition completeness, provider outcomes, actual errors per call, or truth. A semantic reviewer should trace caller→authorization→state/worker/provider→observable result, report source revision, conditions, false/inconclusive cases and untraced gaps, and escalate ambiguous ownership as a finding. It must not infer success from registration, tests, comments or names. The existing error checker and diagrams have separate acceptance criteria, not proof of the paradigm.
