## Working procedure: challenge chunks and attributes against behavior

1. **Identify what can vary and be addressed.** An application can change its endpoint binding without changing its charm's endpoint declaration; a relation composes application endpoints and has its own participants. These are three contracts, not one endpoint-shaped page (`work/endpoint-boundary-111.md:15-24`). Test lifecycle and scope without inferring that a dependent cannot be a useful subject.
2. **Test distinctions between subject, relation and process.** A storage pool is provider configuration, an instance is the resulting identified store, and attachment relates that instance to a unit; detach can remove the relation without destroying the instance (`work/storage-boundary-114.md`; `domain/removal/state/model/storage.go:409-467`). A machine-removal job can affect several of these; its process and completion conditions are not a synonym for any of them (`work/filesystem-lifetime-114.md`). These observations support distinct contracts on the traced paths; they do not prove a universal taxonomy, unique page boundaries or filenames.
3. **Investigate behaviorally relevant facts and their conditions.** Identity/scope and participant relationships can explain what something is; supported records/invariants can explain inspectable state; a stream needs an actual holder and delivery; an actor can explain who reacts; an interaction's caller, preconditions, outcome and timing can change its meaning. Configuration and failures matter where the traced behavior shows they do. These are possible evidence categories, not mandatory attributes, headings or proof that every fact is an interaction. A database-inspector surface and relevance can justify a record; a table name alone cannot.
4. **Separate a request from the effect and its observation.** A machine precheck can refuse merged constraints, and precheck success does not create a ready instance (`domain/machine/service/provider.go:191-219`; `internal/provisionertask/provisioner_task.go:1425-1591`). A status report or audit record observes a process, not proof of its success. Keep action-specific conditions at their action, shared behavior at its subject/mechanism address, and link dependent subjects.
5. **Apply the same derivation to an interface.** A CLI command is a supported request interface with invocation, inputs, output/errors and target-specific consequences; an agent hook command has additional execution-context constraints; a database REPL is a privileged SQL tool. Their structures need not mimic a persistent entity. A generated page is not itself evidence that its command executes or that each generated file is a new system primitive.
6. **Compare the current pages afterwards.** Where source demonstrates distinct contracts in a file, investigate whether separate linkable chunks or a move are justified; do not assume every distinction demands a separate chunk. Preserve anchors and figures until moves are explicitly approved. A reader question tests relevance and coverage, not the boundary or order; physical layout, one-home convention and actual findability remain separately testable.
