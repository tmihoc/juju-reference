## Deriving the chunks before comparing pages

The direct pages `configuration` and `placement` are cross-subject mechanisms: neither is itself a controller/model/application or machine. Their stable homes should explain how settings or a location request participate in those owners' behaviors, not repeat entire owner life cycles. Command pages are independent user invocation interfaces; hook tools have unit/action execution context; config-key catalogues are scoped setting interfaces. Local diagnostics are host tools (and macros invoking them), not API commands. Supported-cloud pages combine several primitives—cloud registration, credentials, controller bootstrap, model settings, compute, network and storage—because readers need provider-specific conditional intersections; shared semantics remain at their respective subject addresses. Reuse snippets transclude pieces of those subjects and do not define independent entities. Similar pages share an interface form only as far as the underlying source contract warrants; the provider-specific and authored exceptions below vary their contents. Page filenames here account for coverage only, never prove these divisions.
